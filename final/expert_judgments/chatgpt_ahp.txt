3. Reason for each criterion's importance
1. AD – Adaptive

Adaptability is important because IoT networks face constantly changing cyber threats, and an AIS should adjust its detection behaviour to recognise new attack patterns.

2. SL – Self-learning

Self-learning allows the AIS to update its detection capabilities automatically without requiring an operator to manually introduce new security rules, reducing maintenance requirements.

3. RES – Resiliency

Resiliency is important because an IoT security solution should recover from attacks, restore normal operations and maintain system availability.

4. DEF – Response/defense

Defense is essential because detecting an attack is not enough; the AIS must take appropriate action to block malicious activity and protect IoT devices.

5. ST – Self-tolerance

Self-tolerance is highly important because reducing false positives prevents legitimate IoT traffic from being incorrectly identified as malicious, avoiding unnecessary resource consumption.

6. LH – Lightweight

Lightweight operation is the highest priority because IoT devices have limited memory, processing capacity and battery power, making computational efficiency essential for practical deployment.

7. DS – Distributed

Distributed processing is important because it allows multiple IoT devices to share security responsibilities, reducing dependence on a central server and improving scalability.
